The Ember Heart

On the Circuit, racing replaced war, religion and most of the economy, and one company owns the track, the broadcast and the bets.

Deep in Cinder Isle's volcano burns the Ember Heart, the last dragon fire on Earth. It keeps the island warm and keeps Ember, the last adult dragon, alive. Live on the broadcast of the Volcano 500, a GearCorp airship rips it out of the mountain. The Heart shatters into seven Shards. The island starts to freeze. Ember starts dying.

Baron Rustwing Gearlock wants every Shard, so he launches the Great Ring Rally, the richest race in history, with checkpoints sitting right on top of them. The world thinks it's watching a race. It's watching a search party.

The Volcano Racers

Six people the Circuit chewed up and spat out.

  • Blaze

    Former world-record sprinter who races on foot. The fastest thing alive, running on a bad knee.

  • Duke Whiskers

    Reality star and cheat with a purple supercar full of illegal gadgets. Every trap he builds backfires on him.

  • Pip #11

    A tiny mouse engineer in giant brass goggles. Anxious, kind and literally overlooked.

  • Jackrabbit Joe #07

    Laid-back ex-champion, two years sober. Owns the Burnt Clutch, a bar he can't drink in.

  • Captain Quackers #02

    The unluckiest duck alive. Explodes, turns to soot and sighs, "Not again..."

  • Ember

    The last dragon, dying a little every issue. A terrifying-looking, gentle, slightly embarrassing dad.

The one rule

The characters can survive anything except each other.

Toon physics

Bodies bounce back. Flattened by an anvil? Pop back into shape a panel later.

Real scars

Nothing emotional bounces back. Debts compound. Lies stick. Grief stays.
