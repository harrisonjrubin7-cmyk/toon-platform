Ember's Flight

Ember is one of the last dragons, and his fire is fading. Fly over Cinder Isle and bring Flicker, Cinder and Spark home.

  • Fly into a lost hatchling to rescue it. Each one following you multiplies your score.
  • Gearlock's drones scare the hatchlings away. With none left to scare, a hit dims Ember's fire.
  • Breathe fire to burn drones, but it costs fire. Catch glowing coals from the vents to stoke it back up.
  • As the fire fades, so does Ember's colour. When it goes out, he has to land.

Fly with the arrow keys or WASD, hold Space to breathe fire, pause Esc. On a phone, use the buttons on screen.
