Blaze's Run

Blaze is the fastest thing alive, and she races on foot. Run as far as you can across the black sand.

  • Jump lava cracks, boulders and GearCorp drones. Hold jump to go higher.
  • Slide under smoke and rockfalls.
  • Grab lightning bolts. Twelve fill the glow: Blaze hits top speed, smashes through anything, and leaves her own shadow behind.
  • Every hit strains her bad knee. Three hits and she has to stop.

Jump ↑/W/Space, slide ↓/S, pause Esc. On a phone, use the buttons on screen.
